Research supervision

Prospective research students

I welcome enquiries from students seeking supervision for a PhD or Master of Research degree, both domestic and international. Supervision is offered in cybersecurity, the Internet of Things, blockchain, artificial intelligence and digital health, and responsible generative AI in education.

Please note: I have limited capacity to support international students seeking Research Training Program (RTP) places, scholarships or other funded opportunities for a research degree at CQUniversity, because these processes are extremely competitive.

Areas of supervision

  • Cybersecurity
  • Internet of Things
  • Blockchain
  • AI and digital health
  • Responsible generative AI in education
Enquire about supervisionPlease include a brief CV, your academic transcripts and a short outline of your proposed research topic.
