Carry these ideas into the Week 6 primer

⚙️Execution path

Solidity is compiled to bytecode, deployed as a contract account, then executed by the EVM.

🗳️Rules before state

Read the caller and stored state, validate with require, then update state and emit evidence.

⏱️Blockchain time

A contract can compare block.timestamp with a stored deadline before allowing an action.
