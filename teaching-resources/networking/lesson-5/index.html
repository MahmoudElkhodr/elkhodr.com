The medium changes the possibilities

Choose a link for the job

A transmitter turns data into a signal, the signal crosses a medium, and a receiver recovers the data. The same basic model works for copper, fibre and radio. The engineering choice depends on what the link must achieve.

Do not memorise one “best” medium. Fibre is excellent for speed and distance, but it is not the answer to every home peripheral. Wireless gives mobility, but a fixed device may benefit from a cable. Requirements decide the technology.

Same devices, different arrangement

Reshape the network

Topology describes how links are arranged between devices. Change the arrangement, then break a link to see how the design affects resilience and complexity.

Recognise star, mesh, bus and ringCompare resilience and costConnect topologies to real examples

Remember

Modern Ethernet LANs are commonly star-based. Large provider networks often use partial mesh so important sites have alternative paths without paying for every possible link.
