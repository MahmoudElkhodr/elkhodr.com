Approach

The training draws on the SAGE framework for responsible generative AI in education, which has been applied across university units in Australia and in international programmes. The central principle is simple: AI may assist, but people remain responsible for their work and must be able to explain and defend it.

Sessions are short on lecturing and long on practice. Participants work through realistic tasks, compare their own judgement with what AI produces, and leave with take-home resources they can keep using.

Working with young people
